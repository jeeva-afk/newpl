Who We Are

Bringing Freshness Home, Every Single Day

Sundari Grocery Store is a family-run grocery store committed to delivering fresh vegetables, fruits, dairy and daily essentials straight to your doorstep. We work directly with local farmers and trusted suppliers so every basket you receive is full of quality and care.

From your morning milk to your evening vegetables, we make everyday shopping simple, affordable and joyful.

  • Handpicked fresh produce
  • Trusted local suppliers
  • Friendly, reliable service
Our Story

From a Small Corner Shop to Your Favourite Store

Sundari Grocery Store began as a small neighbourhood shop with a simple belief: everyone deserves fresh, good-quality food at fair prices.

Over the years, thanks to the love and trust of our customers, we grew from a single counter into a complete grocery destination — now available online so you can shop comfortably from home.

Today, we proudly serve thousands of families and continue to put freshness and friendliness first.
